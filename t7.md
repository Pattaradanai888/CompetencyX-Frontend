## What to build

The end-to-end job drives the two flows the spec left for later through a real browser against a seeded backend: a respondent who already knows their role answers the Skill Assessment until the backend stops asking and lands on the finished view with next topics listed and the "marking requires an account" notice; and a respondent who registers in the browser marks a suggested topic, sees it leave the suggestions and appear in the marked list, then undoes the mark and sees it return. Screenshots are saved on failure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] e2e: choose a role on the start page, answer items until the backend returns no next question, and assert the finished view lists between one and five next topics and shows the signed-out marking notice
- [ ] e2e: register through the UI, open a completed session, mark a suggested topic, assert it leaves the next-topic list and appears in the marked list; undo it and assert it returns
- [ ] Both scenarios run green in the `e2e` job of the CI workflow against the backend's `main`
- [ ] A failing step saves a screenshot to the artifact folder
